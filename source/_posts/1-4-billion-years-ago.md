 科学家们已经证实，大约14亿年前的地球，自转一圈[只要18小时41分钟](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2018/jun/04/the-days-are-getting-longer-but-very-very-slowly "took 18 hours and 41 minutes")，而不是熟悉的24小时。根据“美国国家科学院学报”（the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences）发布的最新计算，[地球每年平均增加0.0000135秒](http://www.pnas.org/content/early/2018/05/30/1717689115 "Earth adds an average of .0000135 seconds to the length of its day every year")，这一趋势将持续数百万年。

资料来源：[http://www.sciencemag.org/news/2018/06/average-earth-day-used-be-less-19-hours-long](http://www.sciencemag.org/news/2018/06/average-earth-day-used-be-less-19-hours-long "美国国家科学院学报")
